High-Demand Support Planning

High-Demand Support Planning

A focused strategy session to prepare your clinic for a spike in enquiries — from a promotion, launch or seasonal peak — before it happens, not during it.

Book a Discovery Call See FAQs

What this covers

  • Mapping expected demand around a promotion, launch or seasonal period
  • Planning response handling so enquiry volume doesn't overwhelm your team
  • Reviewing booking flow capacity ahead of the peak
  • Preparing communication templates in advance
  • Agreeing an escalation plan if volume exceeds expectations
  • A calm, prepared team instead of a reactive scramble

Who this is for

  • Clinics running a seasonal promotion or new treatment launch
  • Practices that have been caught out by demand spikes before
  • Owners wanting a proactive plan rather than firefighting on the day
  • Clinics investing in marketing that will drive a surge in enquiries

Questions

Frequently asked

How far in advance should we plan this?

Ideally two to four weeks before the launch or promotion, so templates and processes are ready in time.

Can you also cover enquiries during the busy period itself?

Yes — this pairs naturally with ongoing Virtual Reception support during the peak.

Is this only for big launches, or does it work for smaller promotions too?

It scales to the size of what you're running — from a single promotion to a full treatment launch.
