Templates & Automation

Templates & Automated Communications

Bespoke, professionally written templates for every stage of the patient journey, so your team never starts a reply from a blank page.

Book a Discovery Call See FAQs

What this covers

  • Enquiry response templates for email, DM and WhatsApp
  • Follow-up sequence templates for enquiries that haven't converted
  • Appointment reminder and confirmation wording
  • Consent and aftercare form messaging
  • Automated DM and WhatsApp greeting set-up
  • A reference library your team can use consistently going forward

Who this is for

  • Clinics with more than one person replying to enquiries, needing consistency
  • Owners tired of rewriting the same replies from scratch
  • Practices wanting automation set up without losing a personal tone
  • Clinics preparing for a busier season and want templates ready in advance
