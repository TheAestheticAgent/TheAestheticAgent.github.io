Virtual Reception

Virtual Reception for Aesthetic Clinics

A remote front desk built specifically for aesthetic clinics — answering enquiries, managing bookings and protecting every patient's first impression, even when you can't get to the phone.

Book a Discovery Call See FAQs

What this covers

  • Answering enquiries across phone, email, DM and WhatsApp as they arrive
  • Responding to prospective patients in your clinic's tone of voice
  • Booking support — scheduling, rescheduling and confirming appointments
  • Following up on enquiries that haven't yet converted
  • Reducing missed opportunities during clinic hours and busy periods
  • Maintaining a consistently professional client experience at every touchpoint

Who this is for

  • Clinic owners who are also the ones injecting, and can't answer the phone mid-treatment
  • Practices where enquiries currently sit unanswered for hours or days
  • Growing clinics that have outgrown ad-hoc, reactive reception
  • Independent practitioners without the volume to justify a full-time hire

Questions

Frequently asked

Is this a call-answering service or something broader?

It covers calls where applicable, but the core of the service is enquiry response across whichever channels your patients actually use — phone, email, DM and WhatsApp — plus the booking action that follows.

Will responses sound like they're coming from my clinic?

Yes. I work from your tone of voice and existing messaging so replies feel like a natural extension of your team, not an outsourced call centre.

How quickly are enquiries answered?

This is agreed with you upfront based on your patient volume and expectations, and reviewed as your clinic grows.
