Booking Experience

Booking Experience Optimisation

A close look at how patients actually find you, enquire and book — so hidden friction stops quietly costing you appointments.

Book a Discovery Call See FAQs

What this covers

  • A full walkthrough of how patients find you across website and social platforms
  • Assessment of your current booking process, step by step
  • Identification of hidden friction points losing you enquiries or bookings
  • Strategic, practical refinements to lift conversion
  • Review of the gap between enquiry and confirmed appointment
  • A written summary of findings and recommended next steps

Who this is for

  • Clinics getting healthy enquiry volume but a lower-than-expected booking rate
  • Practices unsure where in the journey they're losing prospective patients
  • Owners about to invest in marketing who want the booking journey solid first
  • Clinics that have never had this side of the business reviewed externally
