Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

Everything clinic owners typically ask before working together. Can't see your question? Just ask.

What does a virtual receptionist do for an aesthetic clinic?

I answer enquiries as they come in across phone, email, DM or WhatsApp, respond in your voice, guide prospective patients toward booking, and keep your CRM and diary updated — effectively running your front desk without being physically in your clinic.

What does an aesthetic clinic virtual assistant actually cover day to day?

Day to day it typically covers enquiry responses, booking management, diary organisation, follow-ups, CRM updates and general admin — tailored to whichever tier of support you choose.

Can you manage my clinic's bookings?

Yes. I can manage bookings directly within your existing system, confirm appointments, handle reschedules, and make sure your diary reflects reality.

Can you set up my CRM?

Yes — CRM and diary setup is one of my core standalone services. I configure appointment types, durations, booking rules, automated reminders and consent forms so the system runs the way your clinic actually works.

Can you organise my diary?

Yes. Diary structure — appointment lengths, buffer times, treatment categories — is usually one of the first things I tidy up, because it affects everything downstream.

Can you set up automated appointment reminders?

Yes. I configure automated reminders within your existing CRM or booking platform so patients are reminded consistently, without you having to chase manually.

Can you set up consent and aftercare forms?

Yes. I build and configure consent and aftercare forms within your booking system, and set up the automated sending so they reach patients at the right point in their journey.

Do you work with clinics across the UK?

Yes. Support is delivered remotely, so I work with aesthetic and medical aesthetics clinics anywhere in the UK.

Can you support me during busy periods, launches or promotions?

Yes — this is what High-Demand Support Planning covers. We prepare your response handling, booking flow and communication in advance, so a spike in enquiries doesn't overwhelm your team.

Can you manage patient communication end to end?

Yes. From the first enquiry reply through to follow-ups and appointment reminders, I can manage the full communication thread so nothing falls through the cracks.

Can you create templates for my clinic?

Yes. I write bespoke templates for enquiry replies, follow-ups, reminders and WhatsApp or DM greetings, all in your clinic's voice.

Can I use you for ongoing support, or just a one-off project?

Both. Many clinics work with me on an ongoing daily basis for reception and admin; others bring me in for a standalone project such as a CRM setup, booking journey audit, or template suite.

What systems can you work with?

I work across the booking and CRM platforms commonly used in UK aesthetics. If yours is less common, let me know on our call — I'm quick to learn new systems.
