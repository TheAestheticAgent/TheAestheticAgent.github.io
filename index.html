Common questions

Frequently asked questions

What does a virtual receptionist do for an aesthetic clinic?

I answer enquiries as they arrive across phone, email, DM or WhatsApp, guide prospective patients toward booking, and keep your diary and records updated — effectively running your front desk remotely.

Do you work with clinics across the UK?

Yes. Support is delivered remotely, so I work with aesthetic and medical aesthetics clinics anywhere in the UK.

Can I use you for ongoing support, or just a one-off project?

Both. Many clinics work with me on an ongoing daily basis; others bring me in for a standalone project such as a CRM setup or booking journey audit.

What systems can you work with?

I work across the booking and CRM platforms commonly used in aesthetics. If you're not sure yours is supported, ask — I'm quick to learn new systems.
